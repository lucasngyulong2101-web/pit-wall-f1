The grid

Grand Prix points

The top ten score. There is no bonus point for fastest lap any more.

Sprint points

Sprints run about 100 km with no mandatory stop. The top eight score 8 down to 1.

What's new for 2026

  • New power units split power roughly 50/50 between the combustion engine and the electric motor, running on fully sustainable fuel.
  • Smaller, lighter cars with movable front and rear wings. DRS is gone; drivers switch to a low-drag mode on straights.
  • A manual electrical boost replaces DRS as the main overtaking aid when close to the car ahead.
  • Two new names: Audi takes over Sauber, and Cadillac joins as the 11th team.

Reading the timing screen

  • Purple is the fastest time of anyone in the session.
  • Green is a driver's own personal best.
  • Yellow is slower than their best.
  • Red marks a retirement or no time set.

Flags

Yellow: danger ahead, slow down, no overtaking
Green: track clear, racing resumes
Red: session stopped
Blue: let a faster, lapping car through
Chequered: end of the session

A race weekend

Standard weekends: three practice sessions, qualifying on Saturday, race on Sunday. Sprint weekends swap two practice sessions for Sprint Qualifying and a Saturday Sprint.

Qualifying has three knockouts. Q1 drops the slowest six, Q2 the next six, and the last ten fight for pole in Q3.

How this site updates

Every visit pulls the latest calendar, results and standings from the free Jolpica F1 data feed, and an open page checks again every ten minutes. Official results usually appear a few hours after a session ends.
